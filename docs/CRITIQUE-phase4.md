- Fix the coast in three parts: drop the terrain 0.8-1.2 m over the last 3 m of land to create a beach shelf; add a wet-sand band 1.5 m wide at albedo x0.62 and roughness 0.15; replace the thresholded foam with smoothstep(0.35, 0.75, n) over (depth < 0.4 m) times a 2-octave scrolling noise, alpha ramped over 0.35 m, with a 2-3 px shore-parallel bright line.
